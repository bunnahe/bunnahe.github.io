Privacy Policy

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Water Sort ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") — package water.sort.color.puzzle.offline.free.games — handles information when you use it. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or any similar personal details. The App does, however, use a small number of Google (Firebase) services that automatically collect limited technical and usage data, as described below.

Information we collect

1. Data you provide — none required

The App has no sign-up, login, or contact form. Your game progress, level stars, and settings are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to us.

2. Data collected automatically (via Google Firebase)

The App uses the following Google Firebase services, which collect data on our behalf:

ServicePurposeExamples of data
Google Analytics for Firebase Understand how the game is used so we can improve it (e.g. which levels are played, how long sessions last) App interaction events, session data, device model, operating-system version, language/region, app version, a random app-instance identifier, and (where applicable) mobile advertising identifiers
Firebase Crashlytics Detect and diagnose crashes and stability issues Crash logs and stack traces, device state and model, OS version, and a random installation identifier
Firebase Cloud Messaging Deliver optional notifications from us to the app A device registration token
Firebase Remote Config Enable/disable features and adjust settings without an app update A random app-instance identifier and basic device attributes used to fetch configuration

This data is processed by Google as our service provider. It may include device identifiers and coarse location inferred from your IP address. We do not use it to identify you personally.

3. On-device features (no data leaves your device)

  • Local reminder notifications: the App can schedule "come back and play" reminders on your device. These are generated and stored locally; their content is not sent to us.
  • Rate-us prompt: the App may ask you to rate it. This is handled entirely by the Google Play in-app review service; we do not see your rating or review.
  • Local storage: your progress and settings are saved on your device using standard app storage.

Permissions we use

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to show the optional reminder and message notifications. You can deny or revoke this at any time in your device settings.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — so any reminders you have scheduled are restored after your device restarts.
  • Internet access — used by the Firebase services above.

How we use the information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the game;
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs;
  • To measure aggregate, anonymous usage and gameplay trends;
  • To deliver optional notifications and configuration updates.

We do not sell your personal information, and the App contains no third-party advertising and no in-app purchases.

Sharing and third parties

We share the automatically collected data only with Google, which provides the Firebase services listed above as our processor. Your use of these services is also subject to Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • How Google uses Analytics data

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Data retention

Analytics and crash data are retained by Google for the periods described in their documentation and our Firebase settings, then aggregated or deleted. Data stored on your device remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall the App.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off analytics/notifications: you can disable notification permissions in your device settings, and clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes locally stored information.
  • Advertising identifier: you can reset or limit your device's advertising ID in your Android privacy settings.
  • EEA/UK (GDPR): you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating and improving the game.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know, delete, and opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information.

To exercise any right, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data transmitted to Firebase is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we and Google take reasonable measures to protect the limited data involved.

International transfers

Google may process the collected data on servers located outside your country, including the United States. Where required, Google relies on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when the updated policy is posted here, with a revised "Last updated" date.
